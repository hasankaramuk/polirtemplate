# Before you arrive
ID: p1
Range: Before September

Paperwork for new students before registration week, plus planning ahead for continuing students.

## Find your Neptun login details
ID: neptun-login
For: BA 1, MA 1
Start: 2026-07-15
End: 2026-08-20
Date text: Summer (sent by email)
Contact: aro
Links: neptun

New students get their Neptun code and login details by email during the summer. Neptun is where you enrol, register for courses and exams, and see your grades and fees. Check your spam folder.
If you have nothing by mid-August, ask the ARO.

## Self-funded new students: pay your first-semester tuition fee
ID: tuition-first
For: BA 1, MA 1
Date text: Within 15 days of your Letter of Acceptance
Key deadline: yes
Contact: finance, intl
Links: faq-accepted, refund
Good to know: g-tuition

Your Letter of Acceptance gives the amount, payment details and an **indicative 15-day deadline**.
- **Need a visa?** Pay **before your visa appointment**, at least one week before it, and book the appointment at least a month before you plan to arrive
- **EEA or visa-waiver countries:** the official deadline is **15 October** (autumn) or **15 March** (spring)
- **Scholarship holders** whose tuition is covered don’t pay. Check your Letter of Acceptance/Award and Neptun
The invoice appears in Neptun → Finances → Invoices the day after you pay.

## Prepare your documents for in-person enrolment
ID: docs
For: BA 1, MA 1
End: 2026-08-28
Date text: Before registration week
Contact: aro
Links: faq-accepted

Bring these to the Academic Registrar’s Office (ARO), **Room 2.53A, 2nd floor, North Building**, at enrolment. The official list is in the TáTK FAQ for accepted students (linked below).
- **Entry qualification, original + photocopy:** secondary school diploma (BA) or bachelor’s diploma (MA)
- Official English translation of the diploma, if it is not in English
- Language certificate, if your programme requires one
- Enrolment sheet, printed from Neptun, **2 signed copies**
- Passport (both, if you have dual citizenship)
- One 4×4 cm passport photo
- Residence permit and Hungarian tax card, if you already have them (originals + copies)
- Under 18? A signed authorisation from your legal guardian
No apostille or notarisation is needed for the originals.

## Sort out your visa or residence permit
ID: visa
For: BA 1, MA 1
End: 2026-08-14
Date text: As early as possible
Key deadline: yes
International only: yes
Contact: intl
Links: visa-noneea, visa-eea, enter-hungary

**Non-EEA (visa required):** apply for the Residence Permit for the Purpose of Studies at a Hungarian consulate before you travel. A D-visa lets you enter once and stay 30 days. **Visa-waiver countries:** you can enter with a biometric passport for up to 90 days and apply after arrival. **EEA:** you can enter with your ID card. If you stay more than 90 days, apply for a registration certificate.

Ask them what to do about:

- passivating the semester, if you can’t arrive in time
Also tell your lecturers once you know your courses.

## Read your curriculum: what you take in semester 1
ID: ba-curriculum-1
For: BA 1
End: 2026-08-28
Date text: Before course registration
Key deadline: yes
Contact: dept, mentor
Links: ba-structure, ba-curr-2025

The BA is **180 credits** over 6 semesters (about 30 credits per semester). Students admitted from 2025 follow the 2025 curriculum. Check the official structure page for your admission year.
**Semester 1 compulsory courses (28 cr):** Introduction to Political Science (IRB100), Introduction to Law (IRB101), Introduction to Economics I (IRB102), Reading Academic Texts (IRB103), Awareness Training (IRB104), Introduction to International Law (IRB106), History of European Integration (IRB107), Introduction to the History of International Relations (IRB150).
Many 2nd-semester courses require a 1st-semester course to be passed first (e.g. Academic Writing needs Reading Academic Texts), so take all of them now.

## Read your curriculum and plan your 1st semester
ID: ma-curriculum-1
For: MA 1
End: 2026-08-28
Date text: Before course registration
Key deadline: yes
Contact: dept, director
Links: ma-structure, ma-curr

The MA is **120 credits** over 4 semesters: 58 compulsory · 25 mandatory electives (regional & thematic) · 30 specialisation · 7 optional (any ELTE course).
**Semester 1 compulsory courses (27 cr):** Democratic decision-making (IRm04), International organizations (IRm05), Institutions of human rights protection (IRm07), International development policy (IRm09), European politics (IRm11), Security studies (IRm12), Academic writing (IRm15).
Academic writing (IRm15) is a prerequisite for **every** specialisation, so take it in semester 1.
Check the official structure page for the curriculum that applies to your admission year.

## Plan your 2nd-year courses, including all four Regional courses
ID: ba-plan-2
For: BA 2
End: 2026-08-28
Date text: Late August
Key deadline: yes
Contact: dept, aro, finance
Links: ba-structure, ba-curr-2025

**Semester 3 compulsory courses:** Security Studies (IRB115), Policies of the EU (IRB117), Policy Areas of the EU (IRB118), Foreign Policy Analysis (IRB119), Foreign Policy & Security Issues Seminar (IRB120), Research Methodologies (IRB152).
**Regional Civilization Studies: all four are compulsory.** African & Middle East (IRB140), Asian (IRB141), American (IRB142) and European Studies (IRB143), 5 credits each, recommended in semesters 3–4. Check which ones run in autumn and which in spring, and plan both semesters now. If you can’t fit them all into semesters 3–4, ask the ARO about semester 5. All four must be completed before you graduate.
Check you passed the prerequisites (e.g. IRB115 needs IRB111), and check Neptun → Finances for unpaid items, which can block registration.

## Plan your final year: credits, thesis, thematic courses
ID: plan-3
For: BA 3
End: 2026-08-28
Date text: Late August
Key deadline: yes
Contact: aro, dept
Links: ba-structure, ba-curr-2025

Count your credits against the 180 needed. Your final year includes:
- **Thesis Consultation (IRB138.C)** in semester 5 and **Thesis Seminar (IRB138.S)** in semester 6
- **Thematic courses** (lecture and seminar), which have limited places
- any missing **Regional Civilization courses** (all four are required), electives, the **Internship (IRB137)** if not yet done, and International Economics (IRB116)
Missing credits or unsure what still counts? Ask the ARO **now**, not in spring.

## Register for your autumn courses in Neptun
ID: course-reg-aut
For: All
Start: 2026-08-31
End: 2026-09-11
Key deadline: yes
To be confirmed: yes
Contact: aro, dept
Links: neptun-deadlines, faq-enrolled
Good to know: g-registration

Neptun → Subjects → Register for subjects. Registration at TáTK has two phases:
- **Ranking period:** places go by ranking and course capacity. Registering does **not** guarantee a seat
- **First-come, first-served period:** leftover places, plus some courses that only open now
**After the ranking period, check that you are still registered.** If you were dropped, pick an alternative during first-come, first-served.
If a course is full or missing, write to the Department Coordinators. If Neptun shows an error, write to the ARO. Always include your Neptun code and the course code. Exact dates are on the Neptun deadlines page (search “TÁTK”).
