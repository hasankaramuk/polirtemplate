# Registration, exams & studying

## How course registration works (ranking, lucky point)
ID: g-registration
For: All
Ask: aro, dept
Links: faq-enrolled, neptun-deadlines

At TáTK, course registration has two phases:
- **Ranking period:** you register, and places are allocated by your ranking and the course capacity. When the period ends, Neptun “cuts” students below the capacity line
- **First-come, first-served:** remaining places go to whoever registers first. Some courses open only now
If students have the same rank and a course is oversubscribed, Neptun uses a random **“lucky point”**, fixed per student and course. Deregistering and re-registering doesn’t change it.
**What this means for you:** registering once doesn’t guarantee a seat. Check your registered subjects after the ranking period. After the registration period closes, you can’t change your courses without a request (and possibly a fee).
You must be **active** for the semester before you can register at all.

## Exams, offered grades, retakes and fees
ID: g-exams
For: All
Ask: teacher, aro
Being confirmed: yes
Links: faq-enrolled, regulations

- **Offered grades:** some lecturers offer a grade based on coursework. Accept or reject it in Neptun by the deadline. It is never accepted automatically
- **Attempts:** up to **3 attempts per subject in one exam period**, and **6 attempts in total**. If you fail the 6th, your student status ends
- **Registering for the same course:** max. 3 times. Not completing it by the 3rd registration ends your student status
- **Deregistering:** up to 24 hours before the exam
- **No-show** without a justified absence: a fee is charged, and it may count as a failed attempt
- **Retakes:** retaking a failed course in a later semester carries an administrative fee. The reviewer also reports a fee for the 2nd retake (3rd attempt) in the same exam period. Check the current fee list in Neptun
- **Credit limit:** above 198 credits (BA) / 132 (MA), an excess-credit fee may apply

## Canvas and Moodle: which one do I use?
ID: g-moodle
For: All
Ask: teacher, it
Links: lms, it-support

ELTE is moving to **Moodle** as its only learning platform. For TáTK:
- **Autumn 2026:** courses may still be on Canvas
- **Mid-January 2027:** students get access to spring courses on Moodle
- **February 2027:** Moodle goes live, and lecturers can run courses only on Moodle
Canvas stays accessible for now, but it may close early for security reasons, so save the materials you want to keep. **Always use the platform named by your lecturer**, and check both if unsure.

## Credit transfer and course recognition
ID: g-credit
For: All
Ask: aro
Links: aro, faq-enrolled

If you studied elsewhere, you can ask for courses to be recognised. Submit a Neptun request with your official transcript and the course descriptions (syllabi). Do it early in the semester, because the decision affects what you still need to take.
For scholarship holders, credits from a previous institution don’t count towards the 36-credit annual minimum.

## BA: Regional Civilization courses (all four required)
ID: g-regional
For: BA
Ask: dept, aro
Being confirmed: yes
Links: ba-structure, ba-curr-2025

The BA requires all four Regional Civilization courses: African & Middle East (IRB140), Asian (IRB141), American (IRB142) and European Studies (IRB143), 5 credits each. They are recommended in **semesters 3–4**. Check which run in autumn and which in spring. Many students finish one in semester 5, but **all four must be completed before graduation**. Ask the ARO if you are unsure about your plan.


# Money, scholarships & admin

## Tuition fees and deadlines
ID: g-tuition
For: All
Ask: finance
Links: faq-accepted, refund

- **New students:** the Letter of Acceptance gives the amount and an indicative **15-day** deadline
- **Visa required:** pay the first semester **before the visa appointment**, at least a week before
- **EEA / visa-waiver students:** **15 October** (autumn), **15 March** (spring)
- **Scholarship holders** whose tuition is covered don’t pay
Pay through Neptun → Finances. The invoice appears the next day. Refund requests have the same deadlines (15 Oct / 15 Mar).

## Stipendium Hungaricum & Diaspora: check-in and payments
ID: g-scholarship
For: All
Ask: intl, scholarship
Being confirmed: yes
Links: sh-info, sh-payment, tax

Each semester you must **verify your presence in Hungary in person** at the International Office, or the monthly stipend isn’t paid.
- First get **active semester status** in Neptun
- Book an appointment as described in your university email. The reviewer reports this is done through the Outlook booking system (“Scholarship Holders personal registration”), Room 228, 2nd floor
- **Autumn:** check in by **30 September** to still get the September stipend (it may be paid with October’s). **Spring:** by the end of February
- If you check in later, payments start only from that month
You also need a Hungarian bank account in Neptun and a tax card.

## Student card and the validation sticker
ID: g-card
For: All
Ask: quaestura
Links: qter, card-guide, faq-enrolled

- Request your card after enrolment, at the start of your first active semester: get a **NEK document** at a Government Office (Kormányablak), then submit the request in Neptun
- The card is only valid with a **validation sticker for the current semester**, free from the Quaestura Office
- An autumn sticker is valid until **31 March**, and a spring sticker until **31 October**
- Without a valid sticker, your student monthly pass isn’t valid either


# Thesis

## Thesis essentials: windows, supervisor, requirements
ID: g-thesis
For: All
Ask: supervisor, aro
Links: thesis-page, thesis-req, ai-policy, citation, thesis-guide, ba-final, ma-final

- **Topic & supervisor windows:** 15 Oct – 15 Nov and 15 Apr – 15 May. **Changes:** by 15 Sep or 15 Feb
- **Submission:** 15 April for June graduation (15 Nov for January). Register for the final exam by 15 March (or 15 Oct)
- **Length (requirements v6, Nov 2026):** BA about 70,000 characters (±10,000), MA about 100,000 (±20,000), with spaces, excluding title page, contents and annexes
- **Annexes:** title page (Annex 1), declaration of authenticity incl. AI-use declaration (Annex 2), AI-use summary table (Annex 3)
The requirements are updated often. **Always download them from the official page** right before you start formatting.

## TDK: do your own research project
ID: g-tdk
For: All
Ask: research, supervisor
Links: tdk

The Conference of Scientific Students’ Associations (TDK) lets BA and MA students present supervised research and get academic feedback. TáTK runs an **autumn and a spring edition**.
- Pick a topic linked to the faculty’s fields
- Find a supervisor **early**
- Develop a research question, plan and method together
- Follow the current call for registration and deadlines. Papers are 20–40 pages, in English or Hungarian
- Present for about 15 minutes at the faculty conference. The best papers go on to the national conference

# Studying abroad

## Options for studying abroad
ID: g-mobility
For: All
Ask: intl, mobility
Being confirmed: yes
Links: erasmus-faq, traineeship-faq, bip, ceepus, intl-office

- **Erasmus+ semester exchange:** main faculty call usually in **February**, second call in **September**. TáTK requires at least **one completed semester** (the university-level rule may say two, so check the current call). SH and DFP holders aren’t eligible
- **Erasmus+ traineeship:** an internship abroad, with different eligibility. See the traineeship FAQ
- **Blended Intensive Programmes (BIP):** short physical stays combined with online work
- **CEEPUS (Central Europe):** semester stays, or **short thesis-research stays of 1–2 months (min. 21 days)**, or 3–5-day excursions. Deadlines: **1 June** (autumn network mobility), **1 November** (spring network and freemover)
Contact coordinators early, because short-term options aren’t all advertised in one place.
